# FIFA FFAR & UK GBE Reference Guide

## 1. FIFA FFAR Article 15 Quick Checklist
- [ ] Agent license validity verified on FIFA Agent Platform (FAP).
- [ ] Representation agreement uploaded within 14 days of signing.
- [ ] Payment routed through FIFA Clearing House (FCH).
- [ ] Remuneration cap applied based on actual negotiated base salary + conditional bonuses.
- [ ] No fee collected from minors under age of 18 unless signing first professional contract.

## 2. GBE League Bands
- **Band 1**: English Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1.
- **Band 2**: Portuguese Primeira Liga, Dutch Eredivisie, Belgian Pro League, Turkish Süper Lig, EFL Championship.
- **Band 3**: Russian Premier League, Brazilian Série A, Argentine Primera División, Liga MX, Scottish Premiership.
- **Band 4**: Czech First League, Croatian HNL, Swiss Super League, Austrian Bundesliga, Greek Super League.
- **Band 5**: Danish Superliga, Polish Ekstraklasa, Swedish Allsvenskan, Norwegian Eliteserien.
- **Band 6**: All other leagues.
